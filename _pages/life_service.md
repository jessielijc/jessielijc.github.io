Outside of academics and research, I value social engagement, hands-on service, and creative exploration. These experiences help me stay connected with people, communities, and the world beyond engineering.

## Social Practice & Volunteering

I actively participate in volunteer service through the **SUSTech Red Cross**, where I have been involved in organizing and supporting campus and community activities. As a certified **AHA HeartSaver**, I am especially interested in first-aid education, emergency response awareness, and public-service activities that make practical skills more accessible to others.

These experiences have strengthened my communication, organization, and teamwork skills, while also reminding me that technology and engineering should ultimately serve real human needs.

## Hobbies & Interests

Photography is one of my favorite ways to observe and document life. I enjoy capturing everyday moments, landscapes, people, and details that are often overlooked. Through photography, I learn to slow down, notice visual stories, and appreciate different perspectives.

I also enjoy exploring new places, learning practical skills, and participating in activities that bring together creativity, curiosity, and community engagement.
