Research Experience

Tactile Friction Estimation for Dexterous Grasping

Jun 2026 – Present

National University of Singapore · Singapore · Advisor: Xingyu Liu

  • Built an NVIDIA Isaac Lab simulation environment for a LEAP Hand equipped with GelSight Mini fingertip sensors.
  • Developed a tactile-image-based estimator of local friction coefficients; incorporated the estimates into reinforcement learning to inform grasp planning and control.

Unified Exoskeleton Control for Multi-Terrain Assistance

May 2025 – Aug 2026

Southern University of Science and Technology · Shenzhen, China · Advisor: Chenglong Fu

  • Developed a unified neural network-based approach to inform exoskeleton assistance from wearer kinematics across level walking, ramps, and stairs, without explicit inverse dynamics modeling.
  • Deployed a lightweight model on embedded hardware for real-time inference.
  • Research outputs: co-first author of the submitted manuscript and co-inventor of the first granted patent listed above.

Gait Estimation for Post-Stroke Rehabilitation

Jan 2026 – Apr 2026

Southern University of Science and Technology · Shenzhen, China · Advisor: Chenglong Fu

  • Developed and deployed a neural network-based framework to estimate affected-limb kinematics in real time from contralateral limb motion for lower-limb exoskeleton-assisted stroke rehabilitation.
  • Research output: co-inventor of the second granted patent listed above.

Adaptive Resistance Control for Exercise Exoskeletons

Jan 2025 – Mar 2025

Southern University of Science and Technology · Shenzhen, China · Advisor: Chenglong Fu

  • Trained deep learning models to estimate electromyographic (EMG) signals from inertial measurement unit (IMU) data and used these muscle activity estimates to guide adaptive exoskeleton resistance during exercise.
  • Research output: co-inventor of the published patent application listed above.
