SplatSLAM

Dense 3D Reconstruction from Monocular Video via Learning-based SLAM and Gaussian Splatting
Jiacan Li · Yuzhen Song · Yiming Ma

A smartphone video becomes a navigable 3D scene: MASt3R-SLAM estimates camera motion and dense geometry, a refinement step removes stray points, and 3D Gaussian Splatting renders new viewpoints.

Experiments

What the comparisons show

The project evaluates camera tracking on indoor benchmarks and reconstruction on campus scenes with different textures and camera paths.

Tracking

Dense visual matching supports camera localization from RGB video without a depth sensor.

Scene texture

Textured rooms reconstruct more completely; blank walls leave sparse or floating geometry.

Capture coverage

A walkthrough gives better unseen views than a short stationary rotation, even when the latter scores well on its training frames.
